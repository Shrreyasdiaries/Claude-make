The first workbench

Élise Vauclair opens a two-window atelier above a bakery, finishing movements for the great houses of Genève.

1921

The minute repeater

Our first grande complication chimes the hours in a cathedral gong, tuned by ear over eleven months.

1972

Steel, made precious

The Royal Dodecagon debuts: a luxury sports watch in steel, priced above gold. The industry laughs. Then it follows.
