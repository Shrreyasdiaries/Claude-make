Practice

We draw light first, walls second, and leave the quiet for last.

Aram was founded in Mumbai in 2009 by Meher Aram and Tomás Reis. Fifty-two architects now work across three studios, on houses, libraries, retreats and the occasional tower. Every project begins with a week on site, measuring the sun before we measure anything else.

    Approach

    Four drawings before a single wall.

    Each project is worked out on one sheet, in this order. Scroll to watch the plan build up.

    01 · Site

    Read the ground

    We survey contours, trees, wind and water by hand. The building has to agree with the land before it can agree with anyone else.

    02 · Light

    Trace the sun

    We plot the sun's path for the solstices and the monsoon. Rooms are placed where the light is kindest at the hour they are used.

    03 · Mass

    Place the weight

    Thick walls go where heat arrives. Courtyards go where air can move. The plan is mostly settled by now.

    04 · Material

    Choose what ages well

    Lime plaster, laterite, teak and board-formed concrete. Materials that look better in twenty years than on opening day.
